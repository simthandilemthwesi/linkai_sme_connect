# LinkAI SME Connect

## AI-Powered SME to Corporate Opportunity Matching Platform

LinkAI SME Connect is an intelligent platform that connects youth-owned SMEs with procurement opportunities, grants, investors, funding programmes, and business support services through AI-powered matching.

Built for the YES AI Hackathon 2026.

---

## Problem

Many SMEs struggle to find relevant opportunities while corporates and funders struggle to identify suitable businesses.

LinkAI SME Connect bridges this gap through AI-powered profile creation, readiness assessment, and opportunity matching.

---

## Key Features

- AI Profile Builder
- AI Opportunity Matching
- Readiness Scoring
- Corporate Supplier Discovery
- Explainable AI Recommendations
- Analytics Dashboard
- SME Growth Insights
